Fixtures of SPEC-ROUTES-001 (card t12)

Every value in routes-*.json is synthetic. The files keep Google's key names and nesting
(routes, duration, distanceMeters, warnings, legs, steps, travelMode, staticDuration,
transitDetails, stopDetails, transitLine, vehicle, stopCount, error) and carry invented values:
stop names "Example Stop A" and "Example Stop B", line names "Example Rapid" and "38R",
warning texts "WARNING-FIXTURE-1" to "WARNING-FIXTURE-3", error messages "Example ... message".
Exception: routes-transit-many-walks.json keeps the step durations (seconds) of one real transit
answer, so the walk-merge rule is tested on the pattern Google really sent; every name in it is
invented. No raw Google answer is stored in the repository (the raw answers of the live block
stay in the git-excluded evidence folder).

live-{transit,walk,bicycle,noroute}-body.json and -mask.txt are the exact requests the app
builds for the four requests of the live block; a unit test compares them with the app output.

Fixtures of SPEC-ROUTELINE-001 (card t45): routes-line-drive.json, routes-line-no-polyline.json,
routes-line-bad-polyline.json, live-line-car-body.json, live-line-walk-body.json,
live-line-bicycle-body.json, live-line-transit-body.json and live-line-mask.txt.
routes-line-*.json are synthetic: every polyline text in them was produced by the test-side encoder
from invented points near the San Francisco test coordinates (no Google answer is stored). The bad
polyline is the drive text with one space inside it. live-line-<mode>-body.json are the exact line
requests the app builds for the live check (polylineEncoding and polylineQuality added); a unit
test compares them with the app output. live-line-mask.txt is the line field mask.
