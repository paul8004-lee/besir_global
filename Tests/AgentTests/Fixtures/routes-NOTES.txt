Fixtures of SPEC-ROUTES-001 (card t12)

Every value in routes-*.json is synthetic. The files keep Google's key names and nesting
(routes, duration, distanceMeters, warnings, legs, steps, travelMode, staticDuration,
transitDetails, stopDetails, transitLine, vehicle, stopCount, error) and carry invented values:
stop names "Example Stop A" and "Example Stop B", line names "Example Rapid" and "38R",
warning texts "WARNING-FIXTURE-1" to "WARNING-FIXTURE-3", error messages "Example ... message".
No raw Google answer is stored in the repository (the raw answers of the live block stay in
the git-excluded evidence folder).

live-{transit,walk,bicycle,noroute}-body.json and -mask.txt are the exact requests the app
builds for the four requests of the live block; a unit test compares them with the app output.
